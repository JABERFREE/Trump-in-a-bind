# Art Direction - Pirate Maze

- **Style**: Vibrant, stylized 3D with a tropical Caribbean feel.
- **Palette**: Azure blues (ocean), golden yellows (sand), lush greens (foliage), and dark browns (ship wood).
- **Environment**: A maze made of tall, weathered sandy stone walls. The floor is white sand or shallow water.
- **Key Assets**:
    - **Maze Texture**: Weathered sandy stone blocks (texture_tile).
    - **Floor Texture**: Tropical sand or shallow water (texture_tile).
    - **Pirate Ship**: Stylized wooden galleon (sprite/billboard).
    - **Characters**: Distinct pirate portraits for the UI (Captain Trump, Rubio, Hegseth, Vance, Witkopf, Kushner).
- **Lighting**: Bright, direct midday sun with soft blue ambient light from the sky.
- **UI**: Nautical themed, with wood textures and parchment-style text boxes.
