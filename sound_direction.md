# Sound Direction - Pirate Maze

- **Music**: High-energy, orchestral "pirates of the Caribbean" style background track. Upbeat and adventurous.
- **SFX**:
    - **Jump**: A quick "whoosh" or "spring" sound.
    - **Trap Hit**: A sharp wooden snap or metallic "clink".
    - **Goal Reached**: A triumphant "ahoy!" or a short brass fanfare.
    - **Switching Character**: A quick chime or splash sound.
- **Playback**: First-gesture unlock (Play button). Mute button in the corner.
